# Plateau candidate prompt set

Built-in ImageGen generated transparent raster candidates, using Trade Road's approved lookout art as a style/material reference unless noted. Each request specified strict overhead RTS perspective, muted weathered rock and earth, a smaller raised center, readable outer collision edge, genuine transparency, a broad stone-free ramp, and no units, structures, text, tiles, or side-on cliffs. The local game preview applied only color matching and geometric placement; none of these candidate sources is installed.

| Candidate | Final asset-specific prompt direction |
| --- | --- |
| `green-trilobe.png` | Distinct three-lobed moss/olive basin hill; compact raised crest; broader rocky outer escarpment; one right-side vehicle-and-infantry ramp; sparse restrained moss, not lush vegetation. |
| `green-dual.png` | Asymmetric two-lobed forest-earth bluff; low weathered dark-gray stone edge with olive erosion; broad stone-free right ramp; no trees or shrubs on the traversable top. |
| `quarry-two-ramp.png` | Narrow, tall diagonal Strip Mine spoil bench with bottom-left and top-right ramp mouths at its extreme ends, a continuous clear interior, and muted sedimentary rock. This geometry-specific revision used an earlier wider quarry candidate as material reference. |
| `snow-slate.png` | Snow-scoured slate bluff with a broad lower ramp, dirty snow only in crevices, cold desaturated stone and ash earth. A targeted edit removed an obstructive central boulder pile while preserving its outline and ramp. |
| `rust-mesa.png` | Compact two-lobed rust-badlands mesa with muted iron-oxide earth, dark fractured stone, modest central terrace, and one broad left ramp. |
| `wet-earth.png` | Dark wet-olive marsh mound with gray-brown rock and root-worn silt, clear traversable top, low outer bluff, and a broad lower-left ramp; no water puddles. |
| `coastal-bluff.png` | Asymmetric salt-gray coastal bluff on land, worn gray-brown soil and restrained lichen, low raised center, broken edge, and one broad right ramp; no seawater in the sprite. |
| `ash-mound.png` | Cold charcoal-gray Boneyard mound of worn slate and dry ash, clear top, broken full edge, and one broad upper ramp; no snow or literal bones. The refined snow-slate candidate was a material/shape reference only. |

All prompts also constrained the entire silhouette to fit a transparent canvas with generous padding and requested shallow, diffuse lighting rather than a dramatic cast shadow. The phrase “about half the visual bulk of Trade Road” guided the compact raised crest; the authored gameplay footprint was not changed.
