Broodfall — Privacy Policy

Effective July 26, 2026

The short version: Broodfall collects nothing. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no ads, no data leaves your device.

Data collection

Broodfall does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or usage data. The game contains no analytics frameworks, no advertising SDKs, no crash reporters, and makes no network requests of its own.

Game saves

Your settings and campaign progress are stored locally on your device and never leave it. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

The optional full-game unlock is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Broodfall never sees your payment details or Apple ID information. Apple's handling of purchase data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Because Broodfall collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
