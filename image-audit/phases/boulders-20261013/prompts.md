# Boulder source prompts

All three full-resolution transparent sources were produced with the built-in ImageGen tool. The downscaled production PNGs preserve their alpha; no CLI/API fallback was used.

## Weathered cluster

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: transparent top-down RTS natural rock prop sprite prototype for Broodfall
Primary request: a small, irregular cluster of 3 to 5 weathered fieldstones, half embedded in dry soil, convincingly natural and grounded at a 110-pixel gameplay footprint.
Style/medium: realistic painterly game asset, restrained texture and detail readable when downscaled; not cartoon or low-poly.
Composition/framing: strict near-orthographic top-down three-quarter view, cluster centered with modest transparent padding, uneven asymmetrical silhouette. The exposed tops and side faces must have physically plausible volume, chipped edges, subtle stratification, tiny fracture lines, dusty deposits, and contact occlusion where stones meet. No terrain tile around it.
Lighting/mood: soft northwest daylight, short soft contact shadows directly beneath stones.
Color palette: muted warm gray, umber and olive minerals compatible with dark weathered earth; avoid saturation.
Constraints: genuinely transparent background; single isolated sprite; no painted outline, no uniform flat facets, no smooth identical round stones, no circular base/shadow disk, no text, no border, no watermark.
```

## Layered outcrop

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: isolated transparent top-down RTS boulder sprite concept, option 2 of 3
Primary request: one broad, low, weathered sedimentary outcrop made of three interlocking natural stones, a distinctly flatter and wider silhouette than a pile of tall boulders.
Style/medium: realistic painterly game asset, grounded geological texture readable at roughly 110 pixels wide in gameplay; weathered layered strata, chipped corners, dirt trapped in cracks, a few restrained mineral flecks.
Composition/framing: near-orthographic top-down three-quarter view, asymmetric horizontal cluster centered with padding; exposed tops and low side faces, no terrain tile.
Lighting/mood: soft northwest daylight and only tiny contact occlusion immediately between stones.
Color palette: muted warm gray, taupe, earth brown; low saturation.
Constraints: genuinely transparent background with clean edges; no cast shadow or circular shadow, no colored halo, no painted outline, no uniformly round stones, no cartoon or low-poly facets, no text, no border, no watermark.
```

## Fractured granite

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: isolated transparent top-down RTS boulder sprite concept, option 3 of 3
Primary request: an irregular cluster of two substantial fractured granite boulders with several small broken fragments nestled at their base; a more rugged, angular silhouette than the first option, but visibly eroded by weather rather than geometric.
Style/medium: realistic painterly game asset, grounded geological texture readable at roughly 110 pixels wide in gameplay; rough crystalline grain, small chipped planes, a few subtle natural fissures and dusty seams.
Composition/framing: near-orthographic top-down three-quarter view, asymmetric compact cluster centered with padding; exposed tops and believable low side faces, no terrain tile.
Lighting/mood: soft northwest daylight and only tiny contact occlusion immediately between stones.
Color palette: muted slate gray and desaturated olive-brown, low saturation.
Constraints: genuinely transparent background with clean edges; no cast shadow or circular shadow, no colored halo, no painted outline, no smooth identical rocks, no cartoon or low-poly facets, no text, no border, no watermark.
```
